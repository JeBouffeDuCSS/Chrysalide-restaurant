I

The concept

A cuisine of the shell

Chrysalide is a fine dining restaurant in the 1st arrondissement of Lyon, focused on a modern approach to crust cooking. We explore this technique in all its forms to concentrate the essence of each ingredient and the transfer of flavours.

Cuisine by Marc Boullier de Branche, trained at Ferrandi Paris.

The terroir

South-east of France
